Built to Eliminate HR & Administrative Overhead

A full suite of tools designed for employees, team leads, and executives.

1-Click Attendance

Employees check in and out with single clicks. Exact timestamps and working hours are computed automatically with zero manual calculation errors.

Smart Leave Quotas

Custom leave types (Sick, Casual, Annual) with year-by-year balance tracking. Prevents duplicate dates and rejects applications exceeding available quotas.

Approval Workflows

Managers receive real-time queues for their department or direct subordinates. Approve or reject with custom feedback notes instantly.

Automated Signals

Approved leaves trigger automated backend signals that immediately mark the employee's attendance as 'On Leave' and deduct their quota.

Auditing & CSV Export

Admins can filter records by department, status, or date range, and export clean CSV reports ready for payroll and compliance reporting.

Granular Role Security

Secure role-based views for Employees, Managers, and Admins backed by Django Mixins and Django REST Framework custom permissions.
